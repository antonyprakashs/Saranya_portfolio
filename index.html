Farmer's Market App

Mobile Application Design • Direct-to-Consumer Platform

My Role: End-to-End UI/UX Designer (User Research, Wireframing, UI Design & Prototyping)

Project Overview

A mobile platform designed to directly connect farmers with consumers, eliminating middleman commissions. This allows farmers to sell their produce at better profit margins while enabling buyers to purchase fresh, organic products at lower, fair market prices.

Key Features & Design Solutions:

  • 🌱
    Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Marketplace: Simple, intuitive navigation for browsing local produce without intermediary markups.
  • ✨
    Accessible Interface: Minimalist UI designed with high visual clarity and high-contrast typography for effortless use by farmers and consumers alike.
  • 📦
    Order & Listing Workflow: Streamlined, step-by-step process for farmers to quickly upload produce, adjust prices, and manage inventory seamlessly.

Deliverables:
